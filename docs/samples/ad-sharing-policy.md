# Northwind Notes: Data Sharing Policy (sample)

This is a fictional policy used to test PolicyAudit. It does not describe a real company.

## 1. What we collect
We collect your email address, display name and the notes you write.

## 2. Advertising partners
To keep the free tier free, we share your email address and display name with our advertising partners.
Advertising partners may combine this data with their own records to show you relevant ads.
You can opt out of advertising by upgrading to a paid plan.

## 3. Everyone else
We do not sell the text of your notes to anyone.
