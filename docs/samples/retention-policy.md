# Northwind Notes: Data Retention Policy (sample)

This is a fictional policy used to test PolicyAudit. It does not describe a real company.

## 1. Account deletion
When you ask us to delete your account, we permanently delete your profile, your notes and your attachments
within 30 days of the request. Backups containing that data are overwritten within the same 30 days.

## 2. What we keep
We keep invoices for seven years because tax law requires it. Invoices contain your name and billing country only.

## 3. Inactive accounts
Accounts with no sign-in for 24 months receive a warning email and are deleted 60 days later.
